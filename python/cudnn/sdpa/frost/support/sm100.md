# SM100 / SM103 SDPA support

[Back to the support index](README.md)

Scope: FE Python graph SDPA engines on compute capabilities 10.0 and 10.3.
Documentation coverage: forward half native geometries and their head-width envelopes,
plus exact D64/D128/D192/V128/D256/D512 MXFP8 owners below. Per-tensor FP8 and
backward are **Pending migration**. Pending documentation is not an unsupported claim.

## Forward

### FP16 / BF16 width envelopes and implementation identity

The existing eight-aligned QK/V widths use explicit implementation engines on
SM100/SM103. Widths select the smallest covering geometry: D32, D64, D128,
D192/V128, D256, then D512. QK and V may differ. Feature-limited D32 declarations
can still advance to D64/D128. D576/V512 remains exact-only; a wider logical
head does not gain a new envelope. Padding uses the covering tile's work.

| Engine / ID | Covering geometry | Main-body limits |
|---|---|---|
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d128_multi_q` / 20535 | D32, D64, D128 | Multi-Q; existing dense, THD and paged combinations below. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d128_single_q` / 20532 | D64, D128, D192/V128 | Single-Q; D192 requires nonpaged, unpacked THD. Small-head THD is split, or unpacked paged D64 with at most 128 query rows. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d64_quadrant` / 20536 | D64 | Dense queries, at most 32 live rows after packing; no THD or bias. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d192_multi_q` / 20531 | D192/V128 | Multi-Q, one or two CTAs; nonpaged THD with one CTA uses single-Q. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d256_lookahead` / 20519 | D256 | 128-row query tile, two CTAs; dense, THD and paged combinations retain common admission. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d256_swap_ab` / 20520 | D128 or D256 | Dense queries, one CTA. D128 requires paged KV and at most eight live columns; D256 uses 16/32-column tiles. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d512_role_split` / 20521 | D512 | 128-row query tile, four-CTA cluster; serves the existing paged and split combinations. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d512_2x2` / 20522 | D512, exact D576/V512 | 64-row query tile; D512 is nonpaged/unsplit and packs whole groups dividing 64. Physical cluster is four CTAs at D512, two at D576. |

These are covering geometries, not independent feature promises. Shared graph
eligibility checks actual dimensions, declared strides, metadata and optional
ports. Mixed FP8 KV, score outputs, THD packing and split restrictions retain
their previous exact or geometry-specific domains. A partial-width THD graph
is not globally rejected: the existing D192/V128 THD envelope is preserved.
Its compact unsplit storage remains supported; independent physical origins
and packed split-KV retain their exact-width checks.
The D256 half bodies do not admit a fused gate or pre-folded scale.

Heuristics retain candidate order and geometry while naming the selected main
body. A fixed engine/knob record cannot switch to another body during lowering.
Old `sdpa_fwd_prefill_sm100` records for these SM100/SM103 domains decline with
an ownership message and require replanning. Other admitted compute capabilities
in the legacy row's range are unchanged.

Enforcement: [width contract](../../fwd/kernels/sm100/half_flavor_contract.py),
[shared eligibility](../../fwd/engines.py), and the local
[D192](../../fwd/kernels/sm100/d192_half_contract.py),
[D256](../../fwd/kernels/sm100/d256_f16_contract.py) and
[wide-head](../../fwd/kernels/sm100/d512_f16_contract.py) contracts.
The small/mid/wide explicit-engine tests check public-record rebuild, selected
body, numerical output and retained replay for native and padded widths.

### FP16 / BF16, small-head combinations

Here D means D_QK = D_V. Dense uses rectangular Q/O declarations; THD uses
ragged token storage. G is the effective packed query-head group, or 1 when
packing is disabled. Physical tiles are distinct from the number of live rows.
All four owners participate in ordinary discovery.

| Engine name / public ID | Head domain and native geometry | Layouts and feature limits |
|---|---|---|
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d128_multi_q` / 20535 | D32, D64, D128 covering geometries; physical query tile 128, one or two CTAs. Native D32 is dense, unpacked, unsplit and one CTA; other D32 configurations use the D64 envelope. | Dense, THD and paged KV subject to the combinations below. THD split-KV uses single-Q. Bias, KV mask and score side outputs are restricted to this owner within the small-half group. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d128_single_q` / 20532 | D32/D64 through D64; D128 and its envelope through D128; physical 128 x 128 tile, one CTA. | Dense and THD, including split-KV. An unsplit small-head THD plan must be unpacked, paged, D32/D64 and have at most 128 query rows. No bias, KV mask or score side outputs. The D192/V128 domain is described above. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d64_quadrant` / 20536 | D32/D64 and their envelopes through D64; physical 128 x 128 tile, one CTA; at most 32 live query rows after packing. | Dense Q, with dense or paged KV. G must divide 32 and 0 < S_Q * G <= 32. No THD, mixed FP8 KV, bias, KV mask or score side outputs. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d256_swap_ab` / 20520, D128 domain | D128 and its envelope; physical query width 16 and KV tile 128, one CTA; at most eight live query columns. | Dense Q with paged KV only; 0 < S_Q * G <= 8, direct grid. No THD, mixed FP8 KV, bias, KV mask or score side outputs. The D256 domain is described above. |

Q/K/V/O normally have one matching FP16 or BF16 dtype, with FP32 softmax.
D128 multi-Q and single-Q also admit matching half Q/O with both K/V in one
FP8 E4M3 or E5M2 dtype when the native prepared binder supports that declaration.
This is the mixed-KV half path; it is not the per-tensor or MXFP8 Q/K/V API.
Optional Stats uses FP32, with natural-log or base-2 values as declared.

### Small-half combination and storage limits

- Noncausal, causal, bottom-right and sliding-window masks, per-sequence lengths,
  sinks, packing and split-KV have shared admission checks. They are not
  independent feature promises: a final engine/knob record must pass
  `analyze_for` and the owner's local contract before compilation.
- Within this group, bias and per-key KV mask use multi-Q on dense, unpaged,
  unsplit and unpacked plans, without score outputs or an epilogue gate.
  Bias has Q's dtype and a contiguous key axis; live outer strides must be
  16-byte aligned. Available tile/cluster geometry and shared-memory capacity
  further constrain admission (`_bias_arm_decline` and the local config).
- Score max / sum-exp side outputs require exact D128, multi-Q, two CTAs,
  dense unpaged queries and unsplit execution. Single-Q and quadrant have no
  corresponding stores. Arbitrary `score_mod` callbacks are unsupported.
- Page sizes must be multiples of 8 and either divide 128 or be multiples of
  128. Paged K/V use compatible page-table declarations and device KV lengths;
  their HND/NHD layouts are validated at binding.
- Dense Q/K/V/O require an innermost contiguous head dimension and aligned,
  nonoverlapping outer strides; B/H/S order and padding may vary. THD graph
  declarations use token-major storage and separate declared ragged origins.
  Runtime rebinding preserves those declarations instead of assuming compact
  storage or reading lengths back to the host.
- Packed Stats and explicitly padded Stats are different declarations. Packed
  Stats can be token-major or head-major; head-major storage must cover the
  actual packed token count without overlap. Eligible multi-Q/single-Q plans
  also support explicitly padded Stats. See [Rule S1](../../AGENTS.md#hard-rules)
  for capacity and packing obligations.
- This table describes graph engines. Standalone admission remains separately
  checked, using the same canonical main bodies.

### Block-scaled MXFP8, D64/D128 and D192/V128

These graph `sdpa_mxfp8` owners use uniform E4M3 or uniform E5M2 Q/K/V,
with UE8M0 input scales for 32-element blocks. Both have a physical
128 x 128 sequence tile and retain their own pipeline/configuration.
No head-dimension padding envelope is admitted.

| Engine name / public ID | Native head domain | Layouts and combinations |
|---|---|---|
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d128_mxfp8_multi_q` / **20550** | Exact D64/V64 with one CTA; D128/V128 with one or two CTAs | D64 is dense, unsplit and unpacked. D128 additionally admits nonpaged THD with two CTAs, dense-Q paged KV, dense PackGQA and split-KV, subject to the checks below. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d192_mxfp8_multi_q` / **20551** | Exact D192/V128 with one or two CTAs | Dense, nonpaged THD and dense-Q paged KV; unpacked only. Split-KV requires two CTAs. |

- Plain O may be FP16, BF16, E4M3 or E5M2. Optional Stats are FP32
  (natural-log or base-2); optional Amax_O is FP32.
- D128 also supports fixed dense, unsplit block-scaled O: E2M1 with
  E4M3 scales per 16 values, or E4M3 with UE8M0 scales per 32 values.
  Block-scaled O cannot combine with packing, THD, paging or shape overrides.
  D64/D192 do not inherit this output capability.
- Noncausal, causal/banded masks, per-sequence lengths and sinks use shared
  admission. THD inputs use packed per-sequence 128-row scale tiles and
  independent data origins. THD cannot combine with paging or packing;
  admitted split and sink combinations are checked for the actual shape.
- Paged KV requires dense Q, device KV lengths and pages divisible by 128;
  K/V scale pools follow their data pools. D64 has no paged or THD path.
- D128 PackGQA requires a whole group dividing the query tile and plain O.
  Split-KV, length padding, scalar tails, masks and sinks are admitted as
  combinations by `mismatch`, not as independent feature promises.
- D128's `causal_exclude_own_block=32` is an operation attribute. It retains
  its causal/mask and layout restrictions and supports the declared packed
  and split variants. It automatically unlocks owner 20550 for this graph;
  other graphs require `CUDNN_FRONTEND_ENABLE_FROST_ENGINES=1` for these owners.
- Old 20512 records for these exact head domains on SM100/SM103 require
  replanning. Neither owner claims `SUPPORTS_SDPA_STATS_STRIDE_OVERRIDE`.

The enforcing [capabilities and shared admission](../../fwd/engines.py),
local [D64/D128 contract](../../fwd/kernels/sm100/d128_mxfp8_multi_q/contract.py)
and [D192/V128 contract](../../fwd/kernels/sm100/d192_mxfp8_multi_q/contract.py)
define the final domain. Detectors include the
[host contracts](../../../../../test/python/sdpa/frost/test_sdpa_fwd_small_mxfp8_implementation_contract.py),
[public record/replay checks](../../../../../test/python/sdpa/frost/test_sdpa_fwd_small_mxfp8_explicit_engines.py)
and [own-block tests](../../../../../test/python/sdpa/frost/test_sdpa_mxfp8_causal_exclude_own_block.py).

### Block-scaled MXFP8, D256

The following row describes graph `sdpa_mxfp8`. Q/K/V must all be E4M3 or
all E5M2, with per-32-element UE8M0 input scales in the supported F8_128x4
layout. O may independently be FP16, BF16, E4M3 or E5M2. Softmax precision
is unset or FLOAT; optional Stats are FP32 in natural-log or base-2 form.
Optional Amax_O is FP32. This row has no block-scaled O epilogue.

| Engine name / public ID | Head domain and native geometry | Layouts and feature limits | Discovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d256_mxfp8_single_q` / **20545** | Exact `(256,256)`, native D256; one CTA with sequence tile 128 x 128 | Dense, nonpaged THD, or dense-Q paged KV; decode and prefill. Noncausal, causal/bands, lengths and sinks subject to the combination limits below. PackGQA is unsupported. | Opt-in with `CUDNN_FRONTEND_ENABLE_FROST_ENGINES=1`; eligibility does not promise first placement. |

There is no head-dimension padding envelope for this implementation.

### Combination and storage limits for the documented MXFP8 row

- **Paged KV:** dense Q only. Pages must contain a multiple of 128 tokens;
  K/V scale pools page with the corresponding data pools. Declare padding and
  device KV lengths. Paged KV combined with THD queries is unsupported.
- **THD:** nonpaged, unsplit and unpacked. Scale storage is packed by sequence
  in complete 128-row scale tiles; physical data offsets do not change those
  logical scale-tile coordinates. Q/K/V/O/Stats can use independent per-port
  origins. Stats use the supported packed token-major or head-major contract.
- **Split-KV:** sink-free dense queries over dense or paged KV. Nonpaged split
  does not support per-batch padding or an uncovered scalar KV tail; THD and
  sink combinations are declined. The plan's partial buffers and combine
  live in caller workspace. Ordinary output dtypes listed above remain valid.
- **Masks and outputs:** sinks are supported on unsplit plans. Bias, arbitrary
  KV masks, score outputs, epilogue gates, mixed input dtypes, FP4 output and
  `causal_exclude_own_block` are unsupported by this row. General graph band
  and length validation also applies.
- **Storage:** declared data and scale strides must satisfy native binding,
  alignment, capacity and nonoverlap checks. Current scale contents are read
  on each execution; scales are not cached as immutable plan metadata. The
  caller provides the workspace reported by the plan and retains bound
  storage for execution and CUDA Graph replay.

### MXFP8, exact D512

Q, K and V share one FP8 type, E4M3 or E5M2, with E8M0 scale factors per
32-element block. O is FP16, BF16, E4M3 or E5M2; a block-scaled O is served by
the D128 flavor only. Both engines are opt-in
(`CUDNN_FRONTEND_ENABLE_FROST_ENGINES`), like the MXFP8 row they split. That
row's `causal_exclude_own_block` unlock serves D128 only.

| Engine name / public ID | Head domain and native geometry | Layouts and feature limits |
|---|---|---|
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d512_mxfp8_slice_recompute` / 20552 | Exact D512/V512; query and KV tiles 128, one CTA. Each 256-column V/O slice reruns QK-softmax-PV. | Dense, THD and paged KV; a page holds whole 128-row scale atoms. Split-KV keeps half-precision partials. No PackGQA. On the retained-P band below it serves only split plans. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm100_d512_mxfp8_retained_p` / 20553 | Exact D512/V512 under top-left causal with left bound 128 (a window of one KV tile) and no widened right band. The second slice reuses the first slice's P and alpha. | Dense without per-batch KV lengths, or nonpaged THD. Unsplit only. |

An explicit record names its body. On the retained-P band, an unsplit record
uses 20553 and a split record uses 20552; everywhere else only 20552 serves.
An eligibility check without knobs admits both owners on that band. Exact
D512 records for the MXFP8 row 20512 are declined and must be replanned. Sinks
under split-KV follow `supports_mxfp8_split_sink`; other head sizes follow their sections above.

## Backward

**Pending migration.** Forward Stats support does not imply backward support.

## Plan selection and execution

Use the shared [graph execution contract](../../../../../docs/utilities/python_graph_and_execution_backends.md).
Candidate ranking selects an engine and public knobs before compilation; the
selected owner does not switch to a different main implementation during build.
Old exact D32/D64/D128 records for 20511 require replanning. Retained non-exact
20511 domains are separate from that invalidation.

Consumers must query `SUPPORTS_SDPA_STATS_STRIDE_OVERRIDE` on the selected plan.
The qualified half THD HN path requires shape overrides, packed head-major
Stats and the matching native binder; mixed-KV, quantized, padded-Stats and
ragged-Q decode legs do not inherit that claim. The note permits a stride
change within the declared domain, not changing Stats packing or dtype.

An explicit public `(engine_id, knobs)` record fixes the D256 single-Q MXFP8 body.
The dense split-P, generic masked and causal fused-correction schedules are
internal variants. Legacy **20512** records for exact `(256,256)` on these
parts must replan; the ID remains registered for its other eligible domains.
Automatic ordering and placement remain centralized heuristic decisions.

The MXFP8 owner does not claim `SUPPORTS_SDPA_STATS_STRIDE_OVERRIDE`;
general THD binding does not establish that permission.
