# SM120 SDPA support

[Back to the support index](README.md)

Scope: Blackwell GeForce-class **cc 12.0 and 12.1** (SM120, SM121), FROST graph
`sdpa`, `sdpa_fp8` and `sdpa_mxfp8` forward. The engine rows declare the 12.x
range (`sm_lo=120`, `sm_hi=129`) and the implementation contracts list cc
12.0-12.9 (`DEVICE_CCS`), but the adapter (`SdpaFwdDslSm120.check_support`)
refuses every device other than cc 12.0 and 12.1. Forward is documented here;
[Backward](#backward) lists the backward engines and their ownership split.
D576 and mixed FP8-KV plans also need a cuDNN Frontend extension that exposes
the matching binder capability (`supports_native_d576`, `supports_d576_envelope`,
`supports_mixed_fp8_kv`); without it `mismatch` declines with a named reason.

`D_QK` is the Q/K head width and `D_V` the V/output head width. THD means
token-packed variable-length storage. GQA/MQA require `H_Q` divisible by
`H_KV`; `G = H_Q / H_KV`. Softmax precision must be unset on every SM120 row
(`softmax_precisions` is empty). Optional Stats are FP32, with natural-log or
base-2 output (`stats_use_log2`).

## Forward

Eight fixed-body engines participate in ordinary discovery; none is opt-in.
They own both native head pairs and their existing padded envelopes (see
[Head envelopes](#head-envelopes)). The three `sdpa_fwd_prefill_sm120*` rows
remain registered for policy and obsolete-record diagnostics. Their
`delegated_envelope` contract declines every transferred shape, including
padded pairs; records naming 20505, 20507 or 20518 must be replanned.

### FP16/BF16

Q, K, V and O are all FP16 or all BF16, except paged mixed FP8 KV below.

| Engine name / public ID | Head domain and native geometry | Layouts and feature limits |
|---|---|---|
| `sdpa_fwd_sm120_f16_general` / **20544** | Native pairs of 16-wide tiles and their 8-aligned envelopes through D256, except pairs padded to `(256,256)`. TILE_M 64/128 (default 128), TILE_N 64/128 | Dense, nonpaged THD, or dense-Q paged KV with `D_QK,D_V <= 128`. Decode allowed. Dense split-KV and PackGQA. Only owner of additive bias (exact `(128,128)`). |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm120_d256_all_compute` / **20537** | Native `(256,256)` and its 8-aligned envelopes. TILE_M 64/128 (default 128), TILE_N 64/128 | Dense or nonpaged THD. Dense split-KV and PackGQA. No paged KV (prefill tiles page only through D128). |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm120_decode_key_group` / **20538** | Every native 16-wide pair through `(256,256)` and its 8-aligned envelopes. TILE_M 16/32 (default 16); TILE_N is the K/V stage width fixed by the head tiles (`decode_kv_stage`, 16-64 keys) | Dense queries only (no THD). Dense or paged KV through D256. Split-KV and PackGQA. `SCHED_NATURAL` only. No bias. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm120_d512_paired` / **20526** | Native `(512,512)` and `(576,512)`, plus their 8-aligned wide envelopes. TILE_M 64, TILE_N 32 | Dense, nonpaged THD, or dense-Q paged KV. Split-KV on dense and paged plans. PackGQA, including subgroups when `G > 64`. |

Two engines own each exact 16-wide pair: the key-group decode body and either
the general body or, at `(256,256)`, the all-compute body. An explicit record
names its body by engine ID, and its TILE_M must lie in that body's domain
(16/32 key-group, 64/128 general and all-compute); otherwise the record is
declined. A record with TILE_M unset runs that engine's default Q tile, 16 or
128, not the legacy row's default. Automatic candidates take the opposite
direction: each proposed TILE_M names the engine (`select_geometry`).

### Per-tensor FP8

Q, K and V share one type, E4M3 or E5M2, with runtime device-scalar
descales and `Scale_O`. O is FP16, BF16, E4M3 or E5M2. Amax_O is produced; a graph that
declares Amax_S is declined. Graph `Scale_S`/`Descale_S` operands are accepted
and do not change the attention gain (`_sm120_fp8_spec`).

| Engine name / public ID | Head domain and native geometry | Layouts and feature limits |
|---|---|---|
| `sdpa_fwd_sm120_fp8_general` / **20542** | Native pairs of 32-wide tiles and their 16-aligned envelopes through D256. TILE_M 64/128 (default 128), TILE_N 64/128 | Dense or nonpaged THD; decode allowed. Dense split-KV and PackGQA. Block-scaled O at exact `(128,128)` only. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm120_d512_fp8_paired` / **20533** | Native `(512,512)` and its 16-aligned wide envelopes. TILE_M 64, TILE_N 64 | Dense or nonpaged THD. Dense split-KV and PackGQA. No block-scaled O. |

Block-scaled O has `D_QK = D_V = 128`, dense untrimmed Q rows, no split and
no PackGQA. Two forms: FP4 E2M1 O with one E4M3 scale per 16 elements, or
E4M3 O with one UE8M0 scale per 32 elements. FP4 O without `sf_o` is declined.

### MXFP8

Q, K and V share one type, E4M3 or E5M2, with E8M0 scale factors per
32-element block in F8_128x4 order. O is FP16, BF16, E4M3 or E5M2; a
block-scaled O is E4M3 with UE8M0 per 32 at exact `(128,128)` (no FP4 O).
`scale_o` is accepted only together with a block-scaled O.

| Engine name / public ID | Head domain and native geometry | Layouts and feature limits |
|---|---|---|
| `sdpa_fwd_sm120_mxfp8_general` / **20543** | Native pairs of 32-wide tiles and their 16-aligned envelopes through D256. TILE_M 64/128 (default 128), TILE_N 64/128 | Dense or nonpaged THD. Dense split-KV. PackGQA except with a block-scaled O. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm120_d512_mxfp8_paired` / **20534** | Native `(512,512)` and its 16-aligned wide envelopes. TILE_M 64, TILE_N 64 | Dense or nonpaged THD. Dense split-KV and PackGQA. |

### Head envelopes

The padded envelopes use the same explicit owner as their covering native
geometry. Each admitted pair uses the smallest covering native geometry; the wide geometries apply only when both
dimensions exceed 256. A pair with one dimension at most 256 and the other
above 256, such as `(512,128)`, is outside every SM120 envelope.

| Precision | Graph head domain | Native geometry and owner |
|---|---|---|
| FP16/BF16 | Each dimension a multiple of 8, `8 <= D_QK,D_V <= 256`, not both multiples of 16 | Both dimensions rounded up to 16-wide tiles (`head_tiles`): engine 20544, or 20537 when padded to `(256,256)`; TILE_M 16/32 selects 20538 |
| FP16/BF16 | `256 < D_QK,D_V <= 512`, multiples of 8, other than exact `(512,512)` | D512/V512 storage, engine 20526 |
| FP16/BF16 | `512 < D_QK <= 576`, `256 < D_V <= 512`, multiples of 8, other than exact `(576,512)` | D576/V512 storage, engine 20526 |
| FP8, MXFP8 | Each dimension a multiple of 16, `16 <= D_QK,D_V <= 256`, not both multiples of 32 | Both dimensions rounded up to 32-wide tiles, engine 20542 or 20543 |
| FP8, MXFP8 | `256 < D_QK,D_V <= 512`, multiples of 16, other than exact `(512,512)` | D512/V512 storage, engine 20533 or 20534 |

For example, half `(120,120)` runs a `(128,128)` tile and `(384,320)` runs
D512 storage, both on 20505; FP8 `(80,80)` runs a `(96,96)` tile on 20507.
THD rides the same envelopes as dense graphs.

### Combination and storage limits

- **Masks and lengths:** every row serves no mask, top-left or bottom-right
  causal, left sliding windows and widened right bands; a bottom-right anchor
  needs a diagonal. Dense padding uses INT32 per-batch lengths. With per-batch
  Q lengths, rows past `seq_len_q` write `O = 0`, `Stats = -inf`. Q lengths
  without the padding mask, and cumulative lengths on dense graphs, are
  declined. KV tails need not be KV-tile multiples (`skv_tile=0`).
- **THD:** nonpaged, unsplit and unpacked on every row; the key-group decode
  body is dense-only. Data are token-major with D contiguous; the adapter
  declines strides TMA cannot express. Half rows accept sequence or cumulative
  lengths; FP8 and MXFP8 rows accept sequence lengths only (`cu_seq_len`
  unset). Stats may be packed token-major or head-major. Per-batch padded THD
  Stats without ragged offsets are half-only (`thd_padded_stats`). Bound
  per-port ragged offsets for Q/K/V/O/Stats are passed to all three SM120
  lowerings (`graph_origin_specs` in `lower_dsl_prefill`).
- **Paged KV:** FP16/BF16 with dense Q and per-batch KV lengths. K/V may use
  HND or NHD pools with independent INT32 page tables; pool capacities must
  match. Page sizes are 8, 16, 32, 64 or multiples of 128. Head dimensions
  (multiples of 8): through D128 on the 64/128-row prefill tiles, through D256
  on the 16/32-row decode tiles, or the D512/D576 envelopes. Split-KV on paged
  plans requires a decode tile or a D512/D576 tile. No paged FP8/MXFP8 queries,
  bias, THD or block-scaled O.
- **Mixed FP8 KV:** FP16/BF16 Q and O with E4M3 or E5M2 K/V storage, paged
  only, dense Q, within the paged head domains above.
- **Split-KV:** dense queries only, `SCHED_NATURAL` only (`validate_params`),
  and no per-batch lengths except on paged decode or D512/D576 tiles. Half
  plans may carry a sink; the combine folds it once. FP8 and MXFP8 sink splits
  are limited to exact `(128,128)`, dense nonpaged, on cc 12.0/12.1
  (`supports_quantized_split_sink`, `supports_mxfp8_split_sink`). No split
  with bias or a block-scaled O.
- **PackGQA:** dense queries only. A group packs when `G <= TILE_M`;
  `TILE_M % G` need not be zero (`pack_gqa_requires_divisible=False`). The
  wide half D512/D576 tiles also pack groups with `G > 64` when
  `gcd(G, 64) > 1`, in subgroups of that size. An unset TILE_M is judged
  at the plan's default Q tile: 16 on the key-group engine, 64 on the
  D512/D576 tiles, otherwise 128.
- **Additive bias:** FP16/BF16 exact `(128,128)` on engine 20544 only: dense,
  nonpaged, unsplit, unpacked, TILE_M 64/128, without a bottom-right anchor.
  Bias is FP32 or the input type with shape `(1|B, 1|H_Q, S_q, S_kv)`,
  contiguous keys and 16-byte outer pitches. Q/K/V/O must bind without a copy.
  No execute-time shape/stride overrides.
- **Dense storage:** Q/K/V/O bind their declared `dense_flex` strides with D
  contiguous, nonoverlapping B/H/S strides and 16-byte outer-stride alignment
  (paged K/V excepted). There is no layout conversion. Dense Stats are
  `(B, H_Q, S_q, 1)` in a dense-compatible layout. TILE_CGA_M is 1.
- **Scale and sinks:** host scales may be positive or negative; `attn_scale = 0`
  and tensor `attn_scale` are declined. All rows accept per-head FP32 sinks.
- **Unsupported on every SM120 row:** dropout, score modifiers, ALiBi, block
  masks, RNG dump, `kv_mask`, `score_max`/`score_sum_exp`, `unfuse_fma`,
  `attn_scale_prefolded`, `causal_exclude_own_block`, epilogue gate fusion,
  and Amax_S.

## Backward

SM120 backward engines are registered in the
[backward engines](../../bwd/engines.py) `ENGINE_SPECS`. Forward Stats do not
imply backward support. The FP16/BF16 row `sdpa_bwd_sm120` / **20600** selects
one body per graph from declaration facts (`select_implementation` in the
[SM120 half contract](../../bwd/kernels/sm120/half_contract.py)) and delegates
both bodies to their own engines:

| Engine / ID | Graphs | Body |
|---|---|---|
| `sdpa_bwd_sm120_f16_fused` / **20618** | dense FP16/BF16, `D_QK <= 256` | [fused kernel](../../bwd/kernels/sm120/f16_fused/kernel.py) |
| `sdpa_bwd_sm120_f16_two_subgroup` / **20621** | FP16/BF16 THD, and every `D_QK` in (256, 512] | [SM80 two-subgroup kernel](../../bwd/kernels/sm80/f16_two_subgroup/kernel.py) compiled for SM120 (`api_dsl_sm120_thd.py`, `api_dsl_sm120_wide.py`) |
| `sdpa_bwd_sm120_fp8` / **20616** (opt-in) | per-tensor FP8 | [FP8 kernel](../../bwd/kernels/sm120/fp8/kernel.py) |
| `sdpa_bwd_sm120_mxfp8` / **20617** (opt-in) | MXFP8 | [MXFP8 kernel](../../bwd/kernels/sm120/mxfp8/kernel.py) |

The FP8 and MXFP8 engines are one body each and name it (`implementation`);
both bodies share the operand-access helpers and the delta pre-pass in
[`_fp8_common.py`](../../bwd/kernels/sm120/_fp8_common.py). The fused engine
keeps the row's feature domain, head-dim envelope and
alignment, with its own tile domain (`TILE_M` 32/64/128, `TILE_N` 64/128); the
two-subgroup engine keeps the THD tiles (16/32/64) and the 16 x 16 tile above
D_QK 256. A record for 20600 is declined with "owned by the explicit
implementation engine ..." naming the body and must replan. Both engines
inherit the row's default tiles, candidate position and placement-table
entries (`policy_name`). The remaining feature domains are not
yet documented here: see `_sm120_spec`, `_sm120_fp8_spec` and
`_sm120_mxfp8_spec`.

## Plan selection and execution

An explicit public `(engine_id, knobs)` record fixes one implementation and
replays without consulting candidate selection. Unset knobs in such a record
run `SCHED_NATURAL`, unpacked, unsplit, with the engine's default Q tile
(`canonical_knobs`, `explicit_geometry`). A record for engine 20505, 20507 or
20518 at a head pair an explicit engine owns is declined with "owned by the
explicit implementation engines" and must replan; the same IDs still serve
padded-envelope graphs. No SM120 forward slot is retired.

Automatic candidates for owned pairs come from the legacy rows' historical
policy domain (`sm100_legacy_policy_spec`), which is never offered as an
engine: each knob set resolves to one explicit body before it is returned
(`_recommend_sm100_implementation`). Ordering and placement remain heuristic
decisions, so eligibility does not promise selection ahead of the cuDNN backend.

Use the shared [graph execution contract](../../../../../docs/utilities/python_graph_and_execution_backends.md).
Every SM120 plan launches through the prepared pointer path; compilation
declines a plan without one (`SdpaFwdDslSm120.compile`). Execute-time
shape/stride overrides are admitted by `_prepared_decline_reason`, which
declines them with bias, a block-scaled O or dense MXFP8. On cc 12.0, admitted
override-enabled FP16/BF16 THD plans with packed head-major (HN) Stats advertise
`SUPPORTS_SDPA_STATS_STRIDE_OVERRIDE` when their prepared binder supports it
(`supports_stats_stride_override`). The note permits changing the HN head stride
within the declared storage bounds; it does not permit changing the Stats
packing or dtype. SM121, quantized plans, token-major (NH) or padded Stats, and
plans with shape overrides disabled remain outside this declaration. Consumers
query the finally selected plan and retain their fallback when the note is absent.
