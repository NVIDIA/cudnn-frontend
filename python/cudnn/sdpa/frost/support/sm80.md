# SM80 SDPA support

[Back to the support index](README.md)

Scope: Ampere **cc 8.0 exactly**, FROST graph `sdpa` forward. cc 8.6 and later
parts are declined. Forward coverage is documented here; [Backward](#backward)
lists the backward engines and their ownership split. Q, K, V and O must all be FP16 or all BF16. Softmax
precision is unset or FLOAT. Optional Stats are FP32, with natural-log or
base-2 output. FP8, MXFP8, mixed Q/K/V/O types, tensor-valued `attn_scale`,
`attn_scale_prefolded`, dropout, ALiBi, block masks, `kv_mask`, score side
outputs, the Amax_S output, block-scaled O and epilogue gate fusion are
unsupported. Paged KV also requires a native extension whose
`_SdpaSm80FwdBinder` reports `supports_paged_kv`; this is checked when the
plan is built (`SdpaFwdDslSm80.check_support`).

`D_QK` is the Q/K head width and `D_V` the V/output head width. THD means
token-packed variable-length storage. GQA/MQA require `H_q` divisible by
`H_kv`; ordinary GQA does not require the PackGQA optimization.

## Forward

The three engines below participate in ordinary discovery. Their head domains
partition the SM80 domain, so the head dims of a graph name exactly one engine.
All three share one feature contract; the restrictions below apply to each.

| Engine name / public ID | Head domain and native geometry | Layouts and feature limits |
|---|---|---|
| `sdpa_fwd_sm80_f16_k_prefetch` / **20546** | `D_QK <= 192`, `D_V <= 128`; native D64, D128 and D192x128 | Dense, nonpaged THD, or dense or THD Q over paged KV. Masks/bands, lengths, sinks, bias (dense Q) and decode within the limits below. Split-KV and PackGQA with dense Q only. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm80_d256_kv_prefetch` / **20547** | `D_QK, D_V <= 256` outside the 20546 domain; native D256 | Same as 20546. |
| `sdpa_fwd_sm80_wide_cooperative` / **20548** | `D_QK <= 576`, `D_V <= 512`, with either dimension above 256; native D512 and D576x512 | Same as 20546, with one fixed 32-query, 16-key, four-warp tile. |

Engine **20508** (`sdpa_fwd_prefill_sm80`) is still registered but serves no
graph; see [plan selection](#plan-selection-and-execution).

### Head envelopes

Each dimension is independently a positive multiple of 8. The smallest covering
native geometry, in this order, serves the graph:

| Native storage/contraction geometry | Graph head envelope | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| D64 | `D_QK, D_V <= 64` | 20546 |
| D128 | `D_QK, D_V <= 128`, when D64 does not cover both | 20546 |
| D192x128 | `128 < D_QK <= 192`, `D_V <= 128` | 20546 |
| D256 | `D_QK, D_V <= 256`, when no smaller geometry covers both | 20547 |
| D512 | `D_QK, D_V <= 512`, with either dimension above 256 | 20548 |
| D576x512 | `512 < D_QK <= 576`, `D_V <= 512` | 20548 |

`(64,64)`, `(128,128)`, `(192,128)`, `(256,256)`, `(512,512)` and `(576,512)`
use native widths. Other admitted pairs compile the covering geometry's widths
and mask loads past the declared widths, at that geometry's cost: `(72,40)` uses
D128, `(200,128)` and `(128,136)` use D256, `(264,256)` uses D512 and
`(520,504)` uses D576x512. Non-eight-aligned pairs such as `(100,100)`, and
pairs past the last envelope such as `(584,512)` or `(576,520)`, have no SM80
engine.

### Combination and storage limits

- **Masks and lengths:** noncausal, causal (top-left or bottom-right), left
  windows with or without a causal bound, and right-widened bands. A
  bottom-right anchor needs a causal upper bound, and negative right bounds are
  declined. Dense padding uses INT32 per-batch `seq_len_kv`, with optional
  `seq_len_q` trimming; `seq_len_q` without a padding mask and dense cumulative
  lengths are unsupported. `S_kv` need not be a KV-tile multiple.
- **THD:** nonpaged or paged KV; unsplit, unpacked and NATURAL-scheduled. Q and
  KV lengths are both required, as INT32 per-batch lengths or cumulative
  lengths, never both on one side. Bias is unsupported. Packed ports need D
  contiguous, a head stride of at least `D` when `H > 1`, a token stride
  covering the row span, and head/token strides that are multiples of 8
  elements; the batch stride is not read. Each port may carry its own INT32 or
  INT64 ragged offsets (`B + 1` values, positive multiplier); a THD port without
  offsets keeps its dense batch pitch. Paged K/V pools cannot also carry ragged
  offsets. Stats need ragged offsets and must be packed token-major, or packed
  head-major with a head stride covering the packed Q capacity. Per-batch
  padded THD Stats are unsupported. THD plans need caller workspace for length
  metadata.
- **Paged KV:** dense or THD Q, a padding mask and per-request KV lengths, and
  a positive INT32 `paged_max_seq_len_kv`. K and V use INT32 page tables with
  nonoverlapping batch/page strides. Pool capacities may differ. Page sizes are
  multiples of 8 that divide 128, or multiples of 128. The paged tests exercise
  HND and NHD pools chosen independently for K and V.
- **Split-KV and PackGQA:** dense Q only, over dense or paged KV, in every
  envelope. Sinks, padding and Q-length trimming may split. PackGQA accepts
  any `G = H_q / H_kv`, including groups that cross query-tile boundaries; THD
  PackGQA is declined. Split plans need caller workspace whose base is
  non-null and 16-byte aligned. Split plans require `B`, `H_q` and the split
  count each at most 65535; NATURAL-scheduled and THD grids require `B` and
  `H_q` at most 65535.
- **Dense storage:** Q/K/V/O bind their declared `dense_flex` strides: any
  B/H/S order, D contiguous, nonoverlapping, every stepped B/H/S stride a
  multiple of 8 elements, with 16-byte-aligned runtime base addresses. There is
  no layout conversion; other declarations are declined before compilation.
  Dense Stats are FP32 `(B, H_q, S_q, 1)` in a dense-compatible layout.
- **Bias:** dense Q only, FP32 or the Q/K/V dtype, declared as one compact
  `(1, H_q, S_q, S_kv)` plane: the operand the kernel reads
  (`prepared_sm80.build_spec`). Per-batch, head-broadcast and strided
  declarations are declined (`mismatch`). The accept tests cover the compact
  form, including with split-KV and PackGQA. Bias over paged KV is
  **Pending migration**.
- **Scale and sinks:** host scales may be positive, zero or negative; a plan
  compiles the scale's sign, and an execute-time scale must keep that sign.
  Per-head FP32 sinks compose with dense, THD, paged, split-KV and PackGQA
  plans.
- **Standalone scope:** `SdpaFwdDslSm80` and `sdpa_fwd_wrapper_sm80` have their
  own argument contracts and do not extend graph eligibility. The native
  adapter declines RoPE fusion.

## Backward

The FP16/BF16 row `sdpa_bwd_sm80` / **20601** selects one body per graph
from declaration facts (`select_implementation` in the
[SM80 half contract](../../bwd/kernels/sm80/half_contract.py)) and delegates
both bodies to their own engines. Forward Stats do not imply backward support.

| Engine / ID | Graphs | Body |
|---|---|---|
| `sdpa_bwd_sm80_f16_d64` / **20619** | dense MHA, D_QK = D_V = 64, S_q a multiple of 64 and S_kv of 128, no mask, bias, sink, padding or deterministic request, packed Stats | [D64 kernel](../../bwd/kernels/sm80/f16_d64/kernel.py) |
| `sdpa_bwd_sm80_f16_two_subgroup` / **20620** | every other graph the row admits, THD included | [two-subgroup kernel](../../bwd/kernels/sm80/f16_two_subgroup/kernel.py) |

The D64 rule is the adapter's own fast-path test
(`api_dsl._sm80_d64_fast_path_eligible`) restated on declaration facts. Both
engines keep the row's feature domain; the D64 engine declares no THD fields.
A record for 20601 is declined with "owned by the explicit implementation
engine ..." naming the body and must replan. Both engines inherit the row's
candidate position and placement-table entries (`policy_name`). The remaining
feature domains are not yet documented here: see `_sm80_spec`.

Backward D64, noncausal, deterministic and THD launches require `B <= 65535`
and `H_Q <= 65535`: their native grid uses heads on y and batch on z.
Graph and standalone support checks reject larger declarations before compilation.
Dense causal nondeterministic backward uses the flat LPT grid and has no
65535 limit on those two extents; this does not waive other indexing bounds.

## Plan selection and execution

An explicit public `(engine_id, knobs)` record fixes one body, which builds
without consulting candidate selection. A graph whose head dims belong to
another owner is declined. The build checks that the compiled template, tiles,
scheduler and split count match the record. Legal knob domains:

| Knob | Domain |
|---|---|
| `SCHED_POLICY` | NATURAL, LPT, LPT_L2 for dense Q; NATURAL for THD |
| `TILE_M` / `TILE_N` | D64, D128, D192x128: `{16,32,64,128}` / `{16,32,64,128}`; D256: `{16,32,64,128}` / `{16,32,64}`; D512, D576x512: `{32}` / `{16}` |
| `TILE_CGA_M` | 1 |
| `WARP_SPEC_CFG` / `SWAP_AB` | 0 / unset |
| `SPLIT_KV`, `PACK_GQA` | Subject to the split/PackGQA limits above |

The geometry is that of the graph's covering envelope. A record may leave
`TILE_M`/`TILE_N` unset; the adapter then chooses them from the geometry and
the declared shape. Automatic candidates never set them.

Engine **20508** (`sdpa_fwd_prefill_sm80`) keeps its manifest slot, but its
`delegated_exact` covers every eight-aligned pair of its own head envelope on
cc 8.0, and its architecture range excludes every other device, so it serves
no graph. Its capability row remains the policy from which candidates for the
three owners are generated and placed. A 20508 record is declined; replan.

Automatic discovery proposes only the owner of the graph's head dims. The SM80
engines are listed in `TRAIL_ONLY_ROWS`: by default their candidates are tried
only after every cuDNN backend candidate, so automatic selection reaches them
when the backend declines the graph.
Generated placement-table entries, recorded under `sdpa_fwd_prefill_sm80`, can
place specific graph classes ahead of the backend. With
`CUDNN_FRONTEND_ENABLE_FROST_ENGINES` set, FROST candidates lead.

Use the shared [graph execution contract](../../../../../docs/utilities/python_graph_and_execution_backends.md).
Dense plans bind runtime operands through the prepared pointer launch; THD
plans launch the compiled packed host. Execute-time shape/stride overrides are
unsupported, and these engines do not advertise
`SUPPORTS_SDPA_STATS_STRIDE_OVERRIDE`.
