# SM107 SDPA support

[Back to the support index](README.md)

Scope: Rubin **cc 10.7 exactly**, native wide-head FP16/BF16 forward and
per-tensor FP8 forward at D64, D128, D192/V128, D256 and D512. Other half
head widths, MXFP8 and backward are **Pending migration** in this reference.
Pending documentation does not imply that a feature is unsupported.
